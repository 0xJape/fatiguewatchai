1. Real-time webcam monitoring – captures the driver’s face and eye movements while driving.
2. Eye tracking – detects and tracks the driver’s eyes and facial landmarks.
3. Blink-rate detection – monitors changes in how frequently the driver blinks.
4. Eye-closure detection – measures prolonged eye closure.
5. PERCLOS monitoring – analyzes the percentage of time the eyes remain closed.
6. Gaze-direction detection – monitors where the driver is looking and identifies prolonged or abnormal gaze behavior.
7. Yawn detection – identifies yawning as another possible sign of fatigue.
8. AI fatigue classification – classifies the driver's condition into Alert, Mild, Moderate, or Severe Fatigue.
9. Real-time alerts – provides visual and audible warnings when fatigue is detected.
10. Dashboard display – shows the detected fatigue level and monitoring information.
11. Eye-tracking reports – records information such as blink rate, PERCLOS, eye-closure duration, gaze behavior, and detected fatigue events.
12. Data recording and annotation – produces an annotated dataset from the dashcam recordings.
13. Performance evaluation – evaluates the system using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.
